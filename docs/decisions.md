# Decisions

## Assumptions

Each assumption comes from section 2 of the PRD. A final status is one of
VALIDATED, REJECTED or INCONCLUSIVE. The evidence link must point to a report
or run record in this repository. Until an experiment finishes, the status is
NOT TESTED.

| ID | Assumption | Experiment and issue | Status | Reason | Evidence link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Enough trustworthy, dated, located porcini finds exist. | P1, #3 | INCONCLUSIVE | Switzerland and Austria have 4,747 reliable finds, enough to test weather. Tuscany and the Apennines have 85, too few on their own. Weekly Google searches for "porcini" in Tuscany peak within 14 days of the middle find date in 5 of 7 years, against 2 of 7 for a fixed calendar, so they can check season timing during development but cannot stand in for finds or feed the app. | [P1 report](../reports/p1_observations.md), [run record](../reports/runs/p1_observations/20261010T181005Z-696b43.json), [P1c report](../reports/p1c_flush_signs.md), [P1c run record](../reports/runs/p1c_flush_signs/20261011T080539Z-692d26.json) |
| A2 | Tree and habitat data help at regional level. | P2, #4 and #5 | VALIDATED | A 504-square sample grid can be rebuilt with one command. Terrain, rock type, forest cover and broadleaf or conifer cover at least 98 percent of squares at detail finer than 500 m. The Tuscany forest inventory names beech, chestnut and oak woods at one point every 400 m, but dates from 1985 to 1993 and stops at the regional border. Soil from SoilGrids is too uncertain to separate squares. | [P2 report](../reports/p2_habitat.md), [run record](../reports/runs/p2_habitat/20261010T192011Z-c4f95b.json) |
| A3 | Past weather is enough to judge conditions today. | P3a, #6 | VALIDATED | One command rebuilds 30 days of rain, temperature, evaporation and soil values before 300 finds, with source and cost, at about 3.2 free Open-Meteo calls per find. Reconstructed past weather runs 5 to 6 days behind, so on a real morning the last 6 days come from weather model values, which exist from 2017. Models disagree by up to 100 mm on recent rain, and rain detail is about 25 km. | [P3 report](../reports/p3_weather.md), [run record](../reports/runs/p3_weather/20261010T195440Z-a38289.json) |
| A4 | A forecast 7 days ahead can be tested without knowing the outcome. | P3b, #7 | VALIDATED | The Open-Meteo Single Runs API keeps every ECMWF IFS 9 km run since 14 March 2024 with its run time. That covers 765 of 4,832 finds, 36 of them in Tuscany, all from 2024 onwards. Older finds have no stored forecast and cannot be tested this way. On 100 finds, rain 1 to 3 days ahead follows the rain that fell closely, and 6 to 7 days ahead the link is weak. | [P3 report, forecasts published in the past](../reports/p3_weather.md#forecasts-published-in-the-past), [run record](../reports/runs/p3b_forecasts/20261011T070425Z-d61547.json) |
| A5 | Weather and habitat signals beat a simple guess. | P4, #8 and #9 | INCONCLUSIVE | Rain of the 2 to 4 weeks before a day points to when porcini appear: in a German forest counted day by day, 95 percent of porcini days had at least 30 mm of rain from 30 to 8 days before, against 78 percent of other days. Weather cannot pick a place inside a 25 km cell. The only model that beat the simple guesses on places learned habitat from finds, mainly flat ground near paths, so it shows where people walk. The final set of 2025 and 2026 is unused. | [P4b report](../reports/p4_benchmark.md), [run record](../reports/runs/p4b_benchmark/20261011T100307Z-c88cd0.json), [Bielefeld run record](../reports/runs/p4b_bielefeld/20261011T101125Z-c3dfaf.json) |

## Decision log

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-10-10 | Use 500 m squares in EPSG:3035 with IDs like `500m_E4321500_N2345000`. | One fixed grid in metres keeps squares equal in area and lets every dataset join on the same ID. |
| D2 | 2026-10-10 | A day runs from midnight to midnight Italian time, Europe/Rome. | Finds, weather and users all follow Italian local days, so one rule avoids shifted dates. |
| D3 | 2026-10-10 | Study the four porcini species as one group: *Boletus edulis* GBIF 5954958, *B. aereus* GBIF 8733688, *B. reticulatus* GBIF 5954691 and *B. pinophilus* GBIF 5954949. *B. aestivalis* is a GBIF synonym of *B. reticulatus* and is included through that ID. iNaturalist taxon IDs are 48701, 333772, 350216 and 335942. | GBIF count queries on this date found 15 *B. edulis* records in all of Tuscany and about 100 for the four species together. One species alone is too few to test anything. |
| D4 | 2026-10-10 | Use two areas. The home area is Tuscany, GADM ITA.16_1, plus Emilia-Romagna, GADM ITA.6_1, south of latitude 44.30 N to cover the Apennine side. Pistoia province, GADM ITA.16.8_1, is reported on its own. The comparison area is Switzerland plus Austria. | The home area has about 130 to 160 separate finds, which may be too few. Switzerland and Austria have about 8,900 records since 2000 in mountain forests closer to the Apennines than northern Europe. A result there shows whether weather predicts finds at all. Tuscany shows whether it holds where the app will be used. |
| D5 | 2026-10-11 | P4 follows the test rules in `docs/test_rules.md`: one test case is a 500 m square on one day, comparison area blocks of 100 km with a 25 km gap, test years 2022 to 2024, final years 2025 and 2026, and success judged by the share of finds in the top 20 percent of forest squares on the same day. | The rules are fixed before any prediction is scored, so no threshold can be bent to fit a result. Finds only show where people found porcini, so only rankings are fair. |
| D6 | 2026-10-11 | P4b scores only cases from 2017 on: tuning 2017 to 2021 with 392 cases, test 2022 to 2024 with 737, final 2025 and 2026 with 656. Gradient-boosted trees are not scored. The rain guess uses the 7 days that end the day before. Weather comes from ERA5 hourly time series of the Copernicus data store, one request per 25 km cell, summed into Italian days. | Set before any prediction was scored. The weather of a real morning exists only from 2017, see A3. Every comparison point needs weather, and each 25 km cell costs one request of about 2 minutes, so ten years keep it to 380 short requests. 392 tuning cases from 5 seasons suit hand-written rules and logistic regression, not a more flexible model. |
| D7 | 2026-10-11 | Add a timing test to P4b: rank each find day against the other days from 45 days before to 45 days after, in the same square, with checks T1 and T2 in `docs/test_rules.md`. | Added after the first test scores and before any timing score. The test that ranks places on one day cannot show whether rain decides when porcini appear, because every find day already follows rain and rain comes in 25 km cells. |
| D8 | 2026-10-11 | Replace the hand-written rules with rules from published knowledge: at least 30 mm of rain from 30 to 8 days before, a cooling of at least 1 degree over the last week, a 14-day mean of 10 to 15 degrees, and altitude from 200 to 2,000 m. Sources are listed in `experiments/p4b_benchmark/config.toml`. | The first rules took the altitude band from tuning finds, which follows where people search. Test scores of the first rules had been seen, so the report shows both versions. The final set has not been used. |
| D9 | 2026-10-11 | Top 10 and top 20 percent scores count a tie between the find and other places as a random tie break. | A hand-written rule gives only a few distinct values, so a find often ties with hundreds of places. Counting a tie as half the span put such finds just outside the top 20 percent every time, so a rule could score 0 even when it was right. A rule that gives every place the same value now scores like a random ranking. Found after seeing test scores. Continuous models are unchanged. |
| D10 | 2026-10-11 | Move every ERA5 temperature from the height of its 25 km cell to the height of the square, at 6.5 degrees per 1,000 m, using the ERA5 ground height of each cell. Add a season rule from 15 June to 15 November. Rules come from published studies only. Finds check the rules roughly but never set their thresholds. | ERA5 gives one temperature for a whole cell of mountains and valleys, so a temperature rule misjudged slopes far above or below the cell. The user decided that rules must follow published knowledge of porcini, because finds only show where people looked. |
