## Problem

The selected area needs more than a number. F11 requires the facts behind the estimate, F12 requires sources, freshness, gaps and real detail, and F18 requires selected-day weather and recent rain. Generic text such as "good conditions" would not explain why this area received its percentage.

The panel must fit the chosen design and remain tied to the map's area, target day and result batch. A slow response for a previous tap must not replace the current area, and a new weather update must not change the explanation underneath an older map result.

Use those distinctions through the API's actual source metadata.

## Proposed change

Open the chosen detail panel when a cell is selected. Present the area identity, whole experimental percentage or unavailable state, selected target date and actual update time. Keep the selected area visible or clearly identified while the panel is open. Closing it returns focus appropriately and does not reset the map's day.

Render the structured contributing facts from #50 as plain translated sentences with their actual values and periods. Show the selected day's weather separately from recent rain. Let the visitor choose 7, 14 or 30 completed-day rain totals, with dates and units. A future selected day must not make the recent-rain total appear to include future rain.

Expose sources, dataset/survey age, missing information and real level of detail. Explain that weather can be shared over several kilometres even though the displayed area is 500 m. Display available drying/frost/hail information only when the response supports it. Preserve source and licence credits and keep the experimental, unvalidated status easy to find.

Handle loading, missing required input, missing optional weather, old results and failed requests distinctly. Ignore responses for a no-longer-selected area/date/batch. A no-estimate area may still show its available source facts; it must not look like zero probability. Establish the selected-destination handoff for favorites and navigation, which #59 and #60 implement.
