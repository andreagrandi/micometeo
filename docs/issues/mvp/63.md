## Problem

The owner will invest in a domain and VPS only after being satisfied with the complete local product. Passing the data and browser checks does not establish that satisfaction. The interface could still feel unfinished or fail to support the intended companion use despite technically meeting individual criteria.

This issue records the owner's final product decision. It must not be closed by an agent because all other issues are closed, a CI job is green or the owner has not replied.

## Inputs

- `docs/product_prd.md`, including the 16 MVP features and "Local MVP acceptance".
- `reports/mvp/local_acceptance.md` from #61 and #62, with real national-data and physical-phone evidence.
- The chosen design and adjustments in `docs/design/decision.md`.
- `docs/local_development.md` and the exact revision and input versions used for the walkthrough.

## Proposed change

Prepare the complete application locally and provide concise startup and viewing instructions. Make the national habitat data ready and fetch the areas of the walkthrough beforehand so the walkthrough does not depend on the owner debugging setup or buying infrastructure.

Walk through the agreed journey: choose an Italian place, inspect today and a future day, understand the experimental percentage and weather, adjust the map, save/reopen a favorite, open navigation and change language. Let the owner assess the curated UI on phone and desktop against the selected design. State actual source gaps and experimental limitations already accepted in the PRD without reopening a field-validation requirement.

Record the owner's explicit decision, date and the revision shown. If they identify missing or unsatisfactory MVP behavior, record the specific objection and keep this issue open while it is resolved and shown again. Do not move a required MVP correction into the roadmap merely to reach acceptance.

After a positive decision, record that the local MVP is accepted. That decision does not itself authorize domain purchases, server provisioning, DNS changes or deployment. Those actions require separate later authorization.
