## Problem

The habitat investigation produced 504 squares around Cutigliano and San Marcello Pistoiese. The product must cover all Italy, including Sicily and Sardinia, with areas a few hundred metres across. Expanding the pilot's bounding box or relying on its Tuscany forest inventory would leave much of the country without useful inputs.

The pilot also found differences between having a value and having reliable evidence. SoilGrids returned values everywhere, but the soil-acidity uncertainty range was larger than the variation across the sample. The Tuscany tree survey dates from 1985 to 1993. Those findings must survive the move to national processing.

## Goal

Produce a repeatable national 500 m grid with terrain and forest inputs, stable identifiers, source records and explicit missing values. Later prediction and explanation tickets must consume the same versioned habitat build.

## Evidence and starting points

- [P2 habitat report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p2_habitat.md), especially "How each value is filled", "Reliability" and "Coverage and gaps". The pilot rebuilt from downloads in about seven minutes; national processing was not measured.
- [Data formats](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/docs/data_formats.md) defines EPSG:3035, a coordinate system in metres, and IDs such as `500m_E4379000_N2331000`.
- [Layer inventory](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/data/p2_layers.csv) records the source versions and licences.
- [P6 report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p6_cost_licence_portability.md) requires replacing GADM boundaries before distribution and retaining Copernicus and regional-source credits.
- Reuse `src/micometeo/finds/grid.py`, `src/micometeo/habitat/` and `tests/test_habitat.py`. The pilot's in-memory raster is not a national processing strategy.

## Scope and child outcomes

| Child | Outcome | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| #41 | Build and store the national grid using redistributable boundaries. | Stable cell IDs, geometry, region coverage and build version. |
| #42 | Add altitude, slope and slope direction from national elevation data. | Terrain values, coverage and source metadata for #47. |
| #43 | Add national forest cover and broadleaf or conifer information. | Forest values and optional dated regional detail for #47. |

Process manageable geographic chunks, reuse downloads and resume interrupted builds. Keep large source files outside Git. A source gap must remain distinguishable from valid open land. Drawing 500 m squares does not imply that every input distinguishes neighbouring squares.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `reports/mvp/grid_coverage.md`, `terrain_coverage.md` and `habitat_coverage.md` account for all Italian regions, islands, omitted cells and source gaps.
- [ ] AC2: Rebuilding the same source versions yields the same cell IDs and values, as checked by the child repeatability commands.
- [ ] AC3: Synthetic coastal, border, missing-pixel and raster-edge cases pass the child tests. A missing source never becomes zero forest or zero altitude.
- [ ] AC4: The build records source URLs, versions, dates, licences and credits. No distributed grid depends on GADM geometry.
- [ ] AC5: #47 can join terrain and forest by cell ID and build version without downloading or interpreting raster files itself.

## Boundaries

Do not add national soil or geology work as an implicit prerequisite. Local builds and data checks require no mushroom field visits, purchases or public deployment.
