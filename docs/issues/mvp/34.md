## Problem

The owner made a curated interface a launch requirement. P5 was a technical demonstration and cannot serve as the product design. The owner also explicitly requires two alternatives before production UI work, one generated by Google Stitch from a prepared prompt and one created independently by the agent.

The MVP is now selected. The alternatives must show those same features so the comparison concerns usability and appearance, rather than different product scopes. A beautiful map screenshot alone does not show whether weather, favorites, dates and error states can be used on a phone.

## Goal

Deliver two inspectable phone and desktop mockups and record the owner's chosen design, including requested changes. That decision is the prerequisite for #55 and the rest of production UI work.

## Inputs

- `docs/product_prd.md`, "Main user journey", "Language and interface quality" and "Mockup epic requirements".
- `reports/Porcini app feature comparison.md`, especially F11 to F14, F18, F23, F39 and F81. The research supplies feature examples, not permission to copy competitors' assets or accuracy claims.
- [P5 phone findings](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p5_phone_map.md), including cramped date controls and the Safari freeze. The mockups must leave room for readable dates and usable map controls.
- [Book Corners](https://github.com/andreagrandi/book-corners) files `templates/base.html`, `assets/css/input.css` and `package.json` inform feasible components. Tailwind and daisyUI defaults alone do not meet the owner's visual-quality requirement.

## Required comparison scope

Both alternatives show a zoomed-out map of Italy that shows only where forests are, estimates once the view is about 40 to 60 km across or closer, a whole experimental percentage, today plus seven dates, search, optional location, selected-area details, weather and 7/14/21-day recent rain, sources and freshness, two background styles, overlay visibility and opacity, browser favorites, external navigation and Italian/English switching.

Each covers initial loading, the short wait while an area's weather is fetched for the first time that day, a selected area, a square without forest, no estimate, an older update and a recoverable error. Include denied location and unavailable browser storage in the state inventory. Show phone and desktop layouts, with translated examples long enough to expose text overflow. A forest or soil exploration tool, account screen or offline-download screen is outside the selected scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `docs/design/brief.md` maps every MVP interaction and required state to both alternatives.
- [ ] AC2: The artifacts under `docs/design/stitch/` and `docs/design/agent/` can be opened or inspected locally. Both have phone and desktop evidence.
- [ ] AC3: `docs/design/decision.md` records the owner's actual choice and requested adjustments. An agent recommendation alone does not satisfy it.
- [ ] AC4: The chosen design has an implementation handoff covering layout, components, typography, colors, responsive behavior and states.
- [ ] AC5: #55 remains blocked until #54 is complete. Backend and data work may proceed independently.

## Boundaries

This epic creates mockups, not production UI. Sample values must be identified as design examples. Google Stitch authorship must be real; if its tool needs user access, report that concrete requirement during #52. Do not buy a subscription or substitute an agent image and call it Stitch. No VPS, domain or publication is required.
