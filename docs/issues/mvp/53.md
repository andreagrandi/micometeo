## Problem

The owner wants a second mockup made directly by the agent, independently of Google Stitch. It must provide a meaningful alternative for the same product journey. A recoloring of the Stitch output or a single decorative map image would not support that decision.

## Inputs

- `docs/design/brief.md` and sample content from #51, including the 16-feature/state checklist.
- `docs/product_prd.md`, "Language and interface quality" and "Mockup epic requirements".
- [P5 phone findings](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p5_phone_map.md), especially readable date controls, map selection and the panel's use of limited phone space.
- Book Corners' template, HTMX, Tailwind/daisyUI and Leaflet patterns constrain eventual implementation. They do not require copying Book Corners' visual identity.

## Proposed change

Create an agent-authored design with its own layout and visual direction. Use the shared content and tasks so the owner can compare it fairly with #52. Choose a locally inspectable artifact. A disposable HTML/CSS prototype is suitable when it helps demonstrate panel, date, favorite and language behavior. Clearly label simulated interactions and sample data.

Design both phone and desktop layouts. Account for the map, legend, selected-area details, eight-date selector, search, deliberate location action, two map backgrounds, overlay visibility/opacity, weather and recent-rain periods, favorites, navigation and language selection. Show the required loading, no-estimate, older-update and recoverable-error states, with denied location and unavailable storage in the state inventory.

Use a coherent type scale, spacing, colors and component behavior. Test long Italian labels and the English equivalents. Percentages and missing states must have text meaning as well as color. Demonstrate how controls remain reachable when details are open and how the layout uses desktop space deliberately.

Keep the prototype separate from the application routes. Its structure can be discarded after design selection. Do not make backend implementation wait for it and do not use this work to bypass #54's owner choice.

## Deliverables

- `docs/design/agent/README.md` with the design rationale, artifact path, local viewing command and limitations.
- Inspectable phone and desktop views, shared sample content and a completed #51 checklist.
- Source files needed to reproduce the artifact. Temporary visual-check screenshots belong under `/tmp/`.
- Notes identifying the design choices the owner should compare, such as detail-panel placement and how date/layer controls remain accessible.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: The artifact is agent-authored and presents a distinct alternative with the same feature scope and sample content as the Stitch brief.
- [ ] AC2: The documented local viewing method exposes both phone and desktop layouts without connecting to production data or a public Micometeo server.
- [ ] AC3: The checklist identifies every required feature and state, including IT/EN content and the experimental percentage explanation.
- [ ] AC4: Visual inspection at the declared sizes finds no clipped primary controls or hidden core actions when details are open.
- [ ] AC5: The handoff distinguishes working prototype interactions from static illustrations and records any remaining gaps.
- [ ] AC6: Production application routes and UI remain untouched until #54 records the owner's choice.
