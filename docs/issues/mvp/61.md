## Problem

The pipeline tickets test their own behavior, but the MVP needs evidence that their real outputs fit together across Italy. P2 measured 504 habitat squares and P6 estimated requests for the home area. Those results cannot be cited as proof that the national product has complete inputs, stays within quota or publishes consistent eight-date results.

This ticket checks the integrated local data flow. It does not measure mushroom prediction accuracy or ask the owner to validate locations in the field.

- `docs/product_prd.md`, "Coverage, dates and updates" and "Local MVP acceptance".
- `docs/local_development.md`, `weather_updates.md`, `estimate_updates.md` and `api.md`, produced by the implementation tickets.
- Coverage reports from #41 to #43 and request budget from #44.
- [Epic 1 report, Evidence list and Hosting and costs](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/EPIC1_VALIDATION_REPORT.md) identifies which measurements were real, estimated or synthetic. Preserve those distinctions in the new acceptance report.
- #40 supplies check commands. #49 and #50 supply the map and detail endpoints.

## Proposed change

Run the documented local setup, habitat build, weather update and estimate update using real available national inputs. Respect #44's budget before starting downloads and reuse valid local caches. Record the code revision, machine, commands, source/build versions, dates, elapsed time, memory, disk, requests and counted usage.

Create `reports/mvp/local_acceptance.md` with a national-data section. Account for all 20 regions, including Sicily and Sardinia. For each, report expected cells, terrain/forest availability, weather availability, produced estimates and unavailable reasons across all eight dates. Genuine gaps may remain visible, but an implementation that processed only Tuscany must fail this check.

Use representative queries in the Alps, Apennines, mainland lowlands, Sicily and Sardinia. Compare map and detail responses for identical cell/date/batch identifiers. Confirm source dates and credits reach the API. Include outside-Italy and missing-input cases, and a view of all Italy that returns 10 km block summaries within the cap.

Exercise failures in an isolated local test database or fixture run: interrupted weather download, missing required hours, exhausted quota, failed calculation and publication while another page still references the prior batch. Do not damage the retained real national run to stage a test. Verify the last completed results remain usable with their original dates, including a clock advance that makes the last forecast day unavailable.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible local integration checks and a national-data section in `reports/mvp/local_acceptance.md`.
- Tables linking source/build IDs to weather and result batches, regional coverage, resource measurements and failure outcomes.
- Exact commands to repeat the checks and a list of unresolved failures. Link fixes to their owning issues rather than treating a report of failure as completion.
