## Problem

P3 proved access to recent weather and future forecasts, but its client and caches were written for experiments. The current `forecast_hourly` method requests one forecast day. Its request counter counts a retry sequence once, and its cache can retain incomplete recent responses. Those behaviors are insufficient for an automatic national update.

The app needs one shared weather dataset for all visitors, covering recent completed days and eight selectable target dates. Fetching per visitor or per 500 m square would waste the free allowance. Repeating the full historical download every day would also waste calls.

## Goal

Run a daily local job that prepares dated weather for the whole national grid, within the Open-Meteo allowance already assessed in P6. Keep the last completed batch usable when a refresh fails.

## Evidence and starting points

- [P3 report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p3_weather.md) demonstrates 7, 14 and 30 completed-day windows, 23-hour and 25-hour Italian days, source differences and missing forecast hours.
- [P6 report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p6_cost_licence_portability.md), "Open-Meteo for a free app" and "Daily work", records 600 counted calls per minute, 5,000 per hour, 10,000 per day and 300,000 per month. The measured pilot area had 392 weather cells. Its estimate of 1,036 daily calls is not an Italy-wide measurement.
- Reuse `src/micometeo/weather/openmeteo.py`, `features.py`, `tests/test_weather.py` and `tests/test_forecasts.py`.
- Product authority is `docs/product_prd.md`, "Coverage, dates and updates" and "Explanations and weather". Weather refresh is MVP; automatic improvement of the prediction method is deferred F82.

Today means the Italian date in Europe/Rome regardless of the user's language. Today plus seven days means eight dates. Request enough UTC hours to cover their full Italian days. Preserve actual retrieval times, source names, requested model choices and available provider metadata.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `reports/mvp/weather_requests.md` gives national cell counts and separate first-fill and routine-update budgets, including retries and a 31-day month.
- [ ] AC2: Date tests cover both clock changes, year boundaries and target days zero through seven. Recent completed-day totals never include future rain.
- [ ] AC3: Failed, overlapping and interrupted jobs leave the last completed batch intact, as demonstrated by #46's integration tests.
- [ ] AC4: A local documented command performs the same work as the schedule without a VPS or paid API.
- [ ] AC5: Later calculation and API work read stored weather. Map visits cause no weather-provider downloads.

## Boundaries

Use the completed P6 assessment. Do not repeat provider shopping or require archived forecast subscriptions. Historical experiments remain separate from live daily updates. Do not describe modelled weather as a station measurement at a clicked point. No purchases or public deployment are part of this epic.
