## Problem

P3 proved access to recent weather and future forecasts, but its client and caches were written for experiments. The current `forecast_hourly` method requests one forecast day. Its request counter counts a retry sequence once, and its cache can retain incomplete recent responses. Those behaviors are insufficient for the app.

The app needs weather that all visitors share, covering the recent completed days and eight selectable target dates. Fetching per visitor or per 500 m square would waste the free allowance. Downloading the same past days again would also waste calls.

## Goal

Fetch weather on demand, as decided in D15 to D18 of `docs/decisions.md`. The first request for an area on an Italian day fetches the weather of its 0.1 degree weather cells from Open-Meteo once and stores it. Every later request that day reads the stored data. Only weather cells that contain forest squares are ever fetched. Keep the 21 previous days, today and the next 7 days for each cell. A failed fetch keeps the area's last stored weather and estimates with their real dates.

## Evidence and starting points

- [P3 report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/sporameteo/blob/master/reports/p3_weather.md) demonstrates completed-day windows, 23-hour and 25-hour Italian days, source differences and missing forecast hours.
- [P6 report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/sporameteo/blob/master/reports/p6_cost_licence_portability.md), "Open-Meteo for a free app" and "Daily work", records 600 counted calls per minute, 5,000 per hour, 10,000 per day and 300,000 per month.
- A run on 11 October 2026 sent 3,726 places, one per 0.1 degree weather cell with forest, in 38 requests of up to 100 places. Each request asked for 14 days of five hourly variables, took about 0.4 seconds and returned about 1.6 MB. Open-Meteo refused 4 requests as too many after about 2,200 places in 4 minutes, although the run sent at most 500 places a minute. A retry two minutes later worked. Turning the hourly values into Italian days took 0.6 seconds.
- Reuse `src/sporameteo/weather/openmeteo.py`, `features.py`, `tests/test_weather.py` and `tests/test_forecasts.py`.
- Product authority is `docs/product_prd.md`, "Coverage, dates and updates" and "Explanations and weather", together with D15 to D20. Automatic improvement of the prediction method is deferred F82.

Today means the Italian date in Europe/Rome regardless of the user's language. Today plus seven days means eight dates. Request enough hours to cover their full Italian days. Preserve actual retrieval times, source names, requested model choices and available provider metadata.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `reports/mvp/weather_requests.md` gives the number of forest weather cells, the counted calls of a first fetch and of a daily fetch, and the worst case of every cell in one day, including retries and a 31-day month.
- [ ] AC2: Date tests cover both clock changes, year boundaries and target days zero through seven. Recent completed-day totals never include future rain.
- [ ] AC3: Failed, refused and simultaneous fetches leave the stored weather intact, as shown by #46's tests.
- [ ] AC4: A second request for the same area on the same day makes no Open-Meteo call. A zoomed-out map view makes none at all.
- [ ] AC5: Only weather cells with forest squares are fetched. No visitor can make the app exceed the free limits.

## Boundaries

Use the completed P6 assessment. Do not repeat provider shopping or require archived forecast subscriptions. Historical experiments remain separate from live fetching. Do not describe modelled weather as a station measurement at a clicked point. No nightly download of all Italy, purchases or public deployment are part of this epic.
