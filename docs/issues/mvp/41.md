## Problem

All later data joins need one stable national set of areas. P2 built a rectangular pilot of 504 squares and P6 counted 112,814 square centres inside a 28,205 square kilometre home area. Neither is a national product grid. The experiments also used GADM outlines, which P6 found cannot be redistributed in the app.

The owner requires all Italy, including Sicily and Sardinia, and chose areas a few hundred metres across. Retain the existing 500 m convention so research code and product data can join by cell ID.

## Sources and reusable code

- [Data formats, Grid and Coordinates](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/docs/data_formats.md) specifies EPSG:3035 and lower-left-corner IDs such as `500m_E4379000_N2331000`.
- [P2 report](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p2_habitat.md) and `src/micometeo/habitat/grid.py` provide the pilot grid and transformations. `src/micometeo/finds/grid.py` provides point-to-cell assignment.
- [P6, Blockers](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p6_cost_licence_portability.md) identifies ISTAT or OpenStreetMap as replacements for GADM. It did not confirm the licence of a particular ISTAT boundary download.
- `tests/test_habitat.py` already checks alignment and agreement between square centres and IDs. Extend those cases for national boundaries.

## Proposed change

Choose a redistributable national boundary source from the assessed alternatives and record the exact download, version and licence. Confirm the chosen file's terms as part of acquiring that input. Do not reopen general provider or hosting research.

Build 500 m squares aligned to the EPSG:3035 origin and store them in PostGIS through a repeatable local command. Use WGS84 coordinates for map output. Process geographic chunks so the whole Italian bounding rectangle is not materialized as a high-resolution raster.

Make the boundary inclusion rule explicit. Retain cells with positive Italian land intersection so coastal slivers and small islands are not dropped merely because their square centre lies at sea. Preserve the full square ID and geometry, and store its Italian-land intersection or share separately. Region reporting must handle border cells without counting them twice in the national total. The drawing and source-detail explanations remain separate from this geometry.

Each build records its boundary source, parameters, cell count and version. Repeating the same build must update or reuse the same cells without duplicates. An interruption must leave a resumable build, not a misleading completed national grid.

Add an application section to `docs/data_formats.md` explaining the product's
500 m display/join convention and disclosure of coarser inputs. Its historical
experiment rule against finer display must not silently override the later
PRD. Preserve the original research convention and distinguish the two uses.

## Deliverables and handoff

- `reports/mvp/grid_coverage.md` with national and regional counts, boundary inclusion rule, island checks, run time, memory and source credits.

## Acceptance criteria

`uv run pytest tests/app/test_national_grid.py tests/test_habitat.py`.
Record the national command and its output in `reports/mvp/grid_coverage.md`.

- [ ] AC1: Grid tests prove corners are multiples of 500 m, IDs encode the lower-left corner and point-to-cell lookup agrees with stored geometry.
- [ ] AC2: Boundary fixtures cover mainland edges, a coastal sliver, a small island, Sicily, Sardinia and a region border. Valid Italian land is included and sea-only cells are excluded.
- [ ] AC3: The documented national command produces a region coverage table covering all 20 regions, with a distinct national count and no hidden pilot-area filter.
- [ ] AC4: Rebuild and interrupted-build tests preserve IDs, avoid duplicates and do not advertise an unfinished build as complete.
- [ ] AC5: The source record includes the selected file's version, URL, download date, licence and required credits. A dependency check finds no GADM geometry in product outputs.
- [ ] AC6: A coordinate lookup test distinguishes an Italian cell, outside-coverage coordinates and invalid input without substituting a nearby area.
