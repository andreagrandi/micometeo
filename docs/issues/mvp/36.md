## Problem

Individual passing tickets do not prove that the entire product works with national data or that the owner is satisfied with its appearance and behavior. Epic 1 demonstrated this distinction: its API answered correctly while the real iPhone map slowed down and froze.

The owner will not publish to a VPS or invest in a domain and hosting until the complete MVP is accepted locally. Technical checks must support that product decision. They cannot make it on the owner's behalf.

## Goal

Produce one local acceptance record covering real national input processing, daily updates, all 16 MVP features, Italian and English, phone and desktop use, and the owner's explicit decision.

## Inputs

- `docs/product_prd.md`, "Local MVP acceptance", defines completion.
- #30 to #35 supply the application, national data, calculation, APIs and selected UI.
- [Epic 1 evidence](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/EPIC1_VALIDATION_REPORT.md) distinguishes measured pilot results from national work still to verify.
- [P5 real-phone session](https://github.com/andreagrandi/micometeo/blob/master/reports/p5_phone_map.md) records the progressive slowdown after nine successful square requests. A short first-load check would miss this failure.
- `docs/design/decision.md` is the visual reference chosen by the owner.

## Child outcomes

Record the code revision, input versions, machine, browser/device versions, date, commands and observed outcomes. Identify simulated failures and synthetic test inputs separately from the real national run. Missing checks remain missing. A report entry that merely says a ticket is closed is not acceptance evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `reports/mvp/local_acceptance.md` links the national coverage reports and records the complete weather-to-API run, with actual resource and quota measurements.
- [ ] AC2: The report maps all 16 MVP feature IDs to observed behavior in both languages and the relevant phone/desktop evidence.
- [ ] AC3: The real-phone session includes repeated map movement, dates, backgrounds and details without the progressive freeze seen in P5. Its device and duration are recorded.
- [ ] AC4: The report records the owner's explicit satisfaction with the complete local product. #63 remains open if that decision is pending or negative.
- [ ] AC5: No part of acceptance depends on a domain, VPS, paid service, public deployment or mushroom field visits.

## Boundaries

This epic checks software, input data and usability. It does not claim the experimental percentages have measured predictive accuracy. Owner acceptance completes the local MVP; it does not authorize buying or deploying infrastructure. Hosting work requires a later instruction.
