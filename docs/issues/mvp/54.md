## Problem

Two mockups do not settle the product design until the owner has compared them and chosen a direction. The owner made curated appearance mandatory and explicitly reserved this choice before production UI work. An agent recommendation, an unanswered question or completion of the two artifact tickets cannot stand in for that decision.

## Inputs

- Google Stitch artifact and checklist from #52.
- Independent agent artifact and checklist from #53.
- Shared tasks, sample content and state inventory from `docs/design/brief.md`.
- `docs/product_prd.md`, "Mockup epic requirements" and "Language and interface quality".

## Proposed change

Prepare a side-by-side comparison using the same phone and desktop tasks. Show the main map, date selection, area explanation/weather, background controls, favorites and navigation. Include a missing-data state and the Italian/English differences. Identify concrete strengths and gaps in each alternative, such as a hidden opacity control or a panel that obscures the selected area.

Present both artifacts to the owner with their viewing instructions. Record the actual choice, which can be one alternative or a specified combination, and all required adjustments. If the owner rejects both or has not decided, record that status and keep this ticket open. Do not quietly choose the easier implementation.

Translate the chosen direction into an implementation handoff. Identify the selected screen versions, responsive layout rules, type/spacing/color decisions, component states, language behavior and which parts of each artifact to keep. Record any unresolved issue that would prevent #55 from following the design. Do not introduce a new product feature during visual selection without an explicit scope change.

## Deliverables

- `docs/design/decision.md` with artifact links, comparison table, dated owner decision and required changes.
- A concrete screen/component handoff for #55, #56 and #58.
- Explicit status of any rejected or deferred visual alternative. Preserve both artifacts for reference.
