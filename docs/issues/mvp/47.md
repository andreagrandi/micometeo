## Problem

The product must display the estimated chance that visible porcini are growing somewhere in a selected 500 m area on a target day. Nothing in Epic 1 calculates that quantity yet. P5 creates synthetic numbers from location and date. P4's `rule_values` returns the count of matched conditions, and a missing value simply meets no condition. Multiplying that count by a constant would not establish a probability and would incorrectly treat missing evidence as unfavorable evidence.

The owner accepts an experimental, unvalidated estimate for a companion app. Launch does not require the owner to collect mushrooms, report finds or run a field study. This ticket must still define the exact calculation and its assumptions, use real terrain, forest and weather inputs, and explain the result honestly.

## Evidence to carry into the method

- [P4, Rules from published knowledge](https://github.com/andreagrandi/sporameteo/blob/master/reports/p4_benchmark.md) and [its configuration](https://github.com/andreagrandi/sporameteo/blob/master/experiments/p4b_benchmark/config.toml) record five starting assumptions: at least 30 mm of rain from 30 to 8 days before, a last week at least 1 degree cooler than the month, a 14-day mean of 10 to 15 degrees, a season of 15 June to 15 November and altitude of 200 to 2,000 m.
- D18 in `docs/decisions.md` replaces the 30-day window. The stored weather covers the 21 previous days, today and the next 7 days. The method uses three inputs: rain from 21 to 8 days before the target day, rain of the 7 days before the target day and the weather of the target day. The Regione Piemonte sheet behind the 30 mm rule says porcini come up 12 to 15 days after that rain. Any temperature comparison, such as the cooling rule, must fit in the 21 stored days.
- These are not equally established. The 1-degree cooling threshold was a stated project choice. The temperature band came from a German beech-forest preprint using a 20-day window, approximated by 14 days in P4. The cooling rule did not separate porcini days at Bielefeld.
- In Bielefeld, 95 percent of porcini days and 78 percent of other days met the rain rule. This supports a useful input, not a 95 percent probability when it rains.
- The fitted place model failed on the final set and largely learned flat places near paths. Do not turn its reported-find ranking into a national growth probability.
- [P2](https://github.com/andreagrandi/sporameteo/blob/master/reports/p2_habitat.md) found uncertain soil values and old regional tree records. [P3](https://github.com/andreagrandi/sporameteo/blob/master/reports/p3_weather.md) found coarse weather and disagreement about recent rain. Both limits must remain visible.
- `docs/product_prd.md`, "Percentage meaning and calculation", supersedes the earlier requirement to validate percentages before release. `docs/test_rules.md` still protects the completed historical benchmark from being silently retuned.

## Proposed change

Write `docs/forecast_method.md` and implement one versioned deterministic calculation alongside reusable code in `src/sporameteo`. Reuse `benchmark/models.py` for input arithmetic where it is correct, not as an unchanged product scorer.

The method document must specify the estimated event, required and optional inputs, exact formula, parameter values, output bounds, rounding and null behavior. Identify which choices come from published sources and which are provisional engineering assumptions. Explain how the formula maps evidence to an experimental percentage. Do not describe that mapping as measured calibration. Choosing and documenting this formula is a deliverable of this ticket; it is not a missing user interview decision.

Review the pilot's June-to-November season and altitude band for a four-species group across Italy before making them national hard exclusions. Record a reason for retaining, softening or omitting each rule. Check calendar boundaries in leap years. Temperature adjustment must follow the weather-source policy from #44. Open-Meteo corrects temperature to the altitude of the weather cell's centre, about 11 by 8 km of mountains and valleys. Move it once from the recorded centre altitude to each square's altitude, and never apply P4's 6.5 degrees per 1,000 m correction twice.

Use the terrain and forest builds from #42 and #43 and dated weather from #45. Only squares with at least 10 percent forest get an estimate, following D17. A missing required input returns an unavailable result with a reason. An optional regional tree value cannot be required nationwide. A valid low value, absent forest and missing forest are distinct inputs. No recorded find in an area is not an absence observation.

Return the percentage together with structured contributing facts from the same calculation. Each fact identifies the input, value, units, period or source and the role it played. These facts must change when the calculation's relevant inputs change. #58 translates and presents them later.

## Deliverables

- Exact method document, versioned configuration and a pure calculation that performs no network or database work.
- Worked examples for wet/cool forest, dry conditions, contrasting habitat, missing required weather, optional regional gaps and a future target.
- Stable output fields for percentage or unavailability, method version and contributing facts, consumed by #48 and #50.

## Acceptance criteria

Add the focused checks in `tests/app/test_forecast_method.py` and run
`uv run pytest tests/app/test_forecast_method.py`. These tests use fixed
inputs and the worked examples in `docs/forecast_method.md`, not new
downloads or another scoring of the final benchmark set.

- [ ] AC1: `docs/forecast_method.md` contains the complete formula and parameter table, source/assumption distinctions and reasons for each national rule choice. No formula or threshold remains a placeholder.
- [ ] AC2: Fixed example tests reproduce the documented whole percentages and contributing facts. Repeating the same inputs and version gives identical results.
- [ ] AC3: Tests reject invalid units, non-finite inputs and out-of-range output. Missing required inputs yield unavailability rather than a false zero.
- [ ] AC4: Date and altitude tests cover leap-year season boundaries, national edge cases and the documented single temperature-adjustment policy.
- [ ] AC5: Changing a relevant input changes its explanation consistently with the calculation. Optional regional gaps do not disable otherwise complete national cells.
- [ ] AC6: The method and response examples state that estimates are experimental and unvalidated. They contain no P5 synthetic scores, rule-count accuracy claims or invented uncertainty ranges.
- [ ] AC7: The delivery report confirms no final benchmark retuning, field-validation gate or automatic learning was introduced. Software checks and documented assumptions are the completion evidence.
