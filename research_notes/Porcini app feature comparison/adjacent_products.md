# Adjacent products, identification, personal records and outdoor tools

Research date: 2026-10-11. This inventory records publicly documented capabilities. I inspected publisher websites, help pages and store listings. I did not install the apps, create accounts, buy subscriptions or test their predictions. A documented feature means the publisher advertises it. Unknown means the checked sources did not establish it. The user has not selected any of these features for the MVP.

## What do Geoticket and Mondo Funghi currently offer?

### Takeaway

Both products now advertise weather-based mushroom maps. Mondo Funghi also documents a private diary, offline maps, learning tools and data export. Its index measures favorable environmental conditions, rather than a measured probability of finding mushrooms. [Geoticket](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geoticket), [Mondo Funghi](https://mondofunghi.it/funzionalita/)

### Cited findings

#### Mondo Funghi

- The app covers Italian territory. It combines recent rain, soil moisture, temperature, elevation, slope direction, forest type and soil acidity. It shows a daily environmental index, a text explanation, recent history and a forecast. It also shows a porcini conditions layer, separate slope and tree-density layers, saved places, colored markers and outing records. The publisher explicitly says its map does not establish mushroom presence. [Publisher App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/mondo-funghi/id6764337695)
- The conditions area is about five kilometres wide. Users can search place names locally, use their current location or tap the map. Measurements show their dates and missing or stale data. The map includes satellite imagery, topography, shaded terrain, contour lines, trails, protected areas and source explanations. The 0 to 100 index compares conditions with five years of history in the same season. A score of 50 means typical conditions. The history chart offers several date ranges and overlays the user's outing results. The source list names Italy's ICON-2I weather model, reconstructed past weather from ERA5-Land, Copernicus forest data, SoilGrids and TINITALY elevation data. ICON-EU supplies fallback weather. [Feature inventory](https://mondofunghi.it/funzionalita/)
- The diary records place, time, outcome, notes, a photo and tentative or confirmed species. It retains available environmental conditions. Local comparisons recall successful outings and species seen around the same dates. [FAQ](https://mondofunghi.it/faq/)
- Offline maps cover 20 by 20 kilometres with topography, contours, shading, slope direction and tree cover. Satellite imagery and porcini conditions need a connection. Downloads show estimated size and support resuming, repair and deletion. [FAQ](https://mondofunghi.it/faq/)
- Places retain an area or exact point. Sharing uses QR codes, text or files. Exact coordinates require explicit inclusion. Reminders are optional. Permit records include authority, number, cost, receipt and expiry, without expiry notifications. [FAQ](https://mondofunghi.it/faq/)
- The learning section has 303 species cards, habitat and seasonal information, lookalike comparisons, explained quizzes, timed practice exams and review of mistakes. Three-photo identification proposes up to three candidates among 288 species. It runs on the phone. The publisher excludes consumption decisions. [Publisher App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/mondo-funghi/id6764337695)
- The publisher says no account, advertising or analytics are required. Places, photos, identification attempts and diary records remain on the device. Place-name search is local. Anonymous downloads still expose normal connection information, such as an IP address, to the hosting infrastructure. System backups can include local records. [Privacy policy](https://mondofunghi.it/privacy/)
- ZIP export includes personal records, photos, settings and study progress. Import replaces existing data after confirmation. Users can delete categories. The FAQ documents Italian content, Android 8 or later and iOS 17 or later. [FAQ](https://mondofunghi.it/faq/)
- Downloading is free. The advertised options are EUR 4.99 once for study tools, EUR 2.99 monthly or EUR 12.99 yearly for Premium, and EUR 29.99 once for all features. Premium has a seven-day trial. [Publisher App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/mondo-funghi/id6764337695)

#### Geoticket

- Geoticket sells permits for mushroom gathering, fishing and outdoor activities. Its website advertises territory selection, purchase history, notifications, a mushroom map, saved gathering places and a digital fishing catch record. [Download page](https://geoticket.it/download-app)
- Geoticket+ advertises current growth predictions for porcini and other mushrooms. Its stated inputs include weather, vegetation, altitude, precipitation and local climate. The app also advertises an altimeter, compass, GPS tracking, a diary of secret places, regulation-change alerts, local alerts, harvest sharing, a community and Mushroom League challenges. These are publisher claims, without accuracy evidence on the checked pages. [Android publisher description](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geoticket)
- The current iOS release notes describe area photos and growth levels in the bulletin, a new incubation state and a map legend. The store confirms iPhone and iPad availability, free download, monthly and annual Geoticket+ purchases, Apple Wallet support and possible location use while the app is closed. The US listing shows USD 17.99 monthly and USD 24.99 annually. Those are store-region prices, not a verified Italian offer. [iOS publisher listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/geoticket/id1475238989?platform=ipad)
- The Android privacy declaration says the app may collect location and personal information and share location, personal information and other data with third parties. The iOS declaration lists contact information used for tracking. These declarations describe different platforms and are not independent audits. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geoticket), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/geoticket/id1475238989?platform=ipad)
- Its public website also offers outdoor experience bookings and tools for authorities and retailers to sell permits. These belong in the full competitor inventory even if the eventual Micometeo scope excludes them. [Geoticket website](https://geoticket.it/)

### Inferences

- The final comparison should treat both apps as direct competitors for weather and mushroom discovery. Their additional learning, permit and community tools remain separate user choices. [Mondo Funghi](https://mondofunghi.it/funzionalita/), [Geoticket](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geoticket)
- Mondo Funghi's number should not be represented as a probability. Its publisher defines a comparison with that area's environmental history. Geoticket uses stronger probability language, but the sources checked do not provide calibration results. [Mondo Funghi index definition](https://mondofunghi.it/funzionalita/), [Geoticket publisher description](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geoticket)

### Gaps

- Geoticket's exact map coverage, grid size, forecast horizon, species list, saved-place visibility controls, export, offline maps and moderation rules remain unverified. Its Italian App Store page returned a rate limit. The US listing supplied current publisher text. Its website yielded useful search-index text but the fetched page required JavaScript.
- Mondo Funghi's exact forward forecast duration and independently measured prediction quality remain unverified. Its store language metadata says English while its FAQ says the interface and educational content are Italian.
- No hands-on checks establish whether either app implements every advertised feature or handles unavailable data as described.

## What do identification apps and observation communities offer?

### Takeaway

These products cover several distinct needs: possible species identification, a personal record, and community review. Their current capabilities differ by platform. Shroomify now also advertises an early weather outlook, so an older identification-only description would miss relevant functionality. [Shroomify](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shroomify-mushroom-id-usa/id1490594715), [iNaturalist introduction](https://www.inaturalist.org/blog/130697-introduction-to-inaturalist-turn-your-curiosity-into-discovery)

### Cited findings

#### Picture Mushroom

- The app accepts new or existing photos and proposes similar species for comparison. It provides an encyclopedia with habitat, identifying details and toxicity, educational articles, saved foraging places, local seasonal suggestions, a mushroom distribution map, personal collections and sharing. The yearly Premium offer advertises unlimited identification, expert consultation and ad-free information. Downloading is free, with paid purchases. iPhone and iPad are supported. [Publisher App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picture-mushroom-identifier/id1474578078)
- The publisher provides Android and iPhone downloads and a web encyclopedia. [Product website](https://picturemushroom.com/app)
- Users can delete a saved picture, save a photo to their device and share an identification through another app. The FAQ describes these as individual actions. [Publisher FAQ](https://picturemushroom.com/faq)
- The store lists Italian among the available languages. Its privacy declaration includes precise and approximate location, photos, search history and identifiers linked to the user. It also lists purchases, identifiers and usage data for tracking. [Publisher App Store declaration](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picture-mushroom-identifier/id1474578078)

#### Shroomify

- iOS documents offline photo assistance, illustrated trait questions, advanced identification and name search. Its guide has over 400 species and 1,000 photos. Regional and seasonal records order candidates. Users can compare similar species, save favorites and keep a local photo logbook. Release notes add three-photo comparison, export and restore. [Publisher iOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shroomify-mushroom-id-usa/id1490594715)
- An early preview compares recent and future weather with usual seasonal conditions for supported places and species. It excludes harvest counts and habitat comparisons. Weather requires a connection. iOS costs USD 5.99 with current premium tools included, no separate subscription or ads, and optional photo contributions. It describes English, French, German and Italian content. [Publisher iOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shroomify-mushroom-id-usa/id1490594715)
- The Android listing still describes trait-based matching, a monthly top-20 list, edibility information and regional datasets chosen from the user's position. It lists in-app purchases. Do not assume the newer iOS tools exist on Android. [Publisher Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.mushroom.shroomify)

#### iNaturalist

- iNaturalist provides a free website and mobile apps. Users record organisms worldwide with photos or sounds, date, time and location. Identification suggestions help start the record. The community can refine identification. Uploaded observations follow the account across devices. [Free app downloads](https://www.inaturalist.org/redirect_links/12), [Publisher introduction](https://www.inaturalist.org/blog/130697-introduction-to-inaturalist-turn-your-curiosity-into-discovery)
- Users can search observations on a map by species, area and date. Other filters cover observers, identification status, photos or sounds, projects, licenses and custom observation details. The platform also has species information, projects, journals, a forum and activity on followed observations. [Search guide](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/search%2Burls), [iPhone feature comparison](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000216149-what-s-the-difference-between-inaturalist-and-inaturalist-classic-)
- The current iPhone app documents live camera suggestions, offline suggestions, batch photo import and multiple-photo observations. Its older Classic app has different capabilities. [Official app comparison](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000216149-what-s-the-difference-between-inaturalist-and-inaturalist-classic-)
- Observations are public, but location visibility can be open, blurred or private. The default is open. Blurring replaces the point with a random location in a 0.2-degree cell. Private locations disappear from public geographic searches. Users may grant trusted people or projects access. The service can also hide locations for vulnerable species. The help page warns that nearby public records can reveal a blurred location. [Current location privacy guide](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000169938-what-is-geoprivacy-what-does-it-mean-for-an-observation-to-be-obscured-)
- Account holders can export observation searches as CSV files. [Official download guide](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000170342-how-can-i-download-data-from-inaturalist-)
- Community rules support flagged and hidden content, warnings, temporary or indefinite suspension and bans. Curators, administrators and staff enforce rules, including deliberately false identification and abusive conduct. [Enforcement guide](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000232621-community-guidelines-enforcement)

#### Mushroom Observer

- The Android app records mushroom observations and takes or attaches photos. It saves records without a connection and uploads them later to the user's Mushroom Observer account. The community helps identify specimens. The publisher declares collection of location, personal information and photos, encrypted transfer and a deletion-request option. [Publisher Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=org.mushroomobserver)
- The website currently requires a verified account for nearly all content. It says account creation is free and links to data and image downloads without login. [Current access page](https://mushroomobserver.org/)
- The organization's stated purpose includes recording fungal biodiversity, helping people identify mushrooms and building a community around mycology. It publishes data through the Global Biodiversity Information Facility. [Publisher record](https://www.gbif.org/publisher/b873c239-a68e-4133-ae0b-0c534101f7b5)

### Inferences

- A private journal and a public observation community create different products. Mondo Funghi keeps records locally, while iNaturalist publishes observations and offers controls over location disclosure. This is a product decision for the user, not a detail that should follow automatically from competitor parity. [Mondo Funghi privacy](https://mondofunghi.it/privacy/), [iNaturalist location privacy](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000169938-what-is-geoprivacy-what-does-it-mean-for-an-observation-to-be-obscured-)
- Photo suggestions, field-guide comparison, paid expert consultation and community identification should be separate entries in the feature menu. The checked apps implement different combinations. [Picture Mushroom](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/picture-mushroom-identifier/id1474578078), [Shroomify](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shroomify-mushroom-id-usa/id1490594715), [Mushroom Observer](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=org.mushroomobserver)

### Gaps

- Picture Mushroom's bulk export, offline operation, moderation, exact map data source, geographic prediction coverage and accuracy remain unverified. The distribution map should not be described as a weather-based growth forecast without further evidence. The FAQ's broad privacy assurance is less specific than the store declaration.
- Shroomify's exact weather coverage, forecast duration, logbook export format, map navigation and independent identification accuracy remain unverified. The store descriptions differ by platform. Its store language metadata also differs from its publisher's description of translated content.
- iNaturalist's offline map and trail navigation capabilities were not established. Its iPhone comparison page was updated in July 2025, so differences listed there may lag the newest app release. I did not verify parity with Android.
- Mushroom Observer's detailed website functions, location hiding, moderation, export formats, native iOS availability and pricing beyond free account creation remain unverified because public pages required login. I did not create an account.
- None of the checked sources establishes a reliable mushroom consumption decision from an app's identification alone. No species-identification accuracy test was performed.

## Which outdoor mapping features provide an adjacent reference?

### Takeaway

AllTrails provides a separate comparison for planning a visit and navigating it. Its tools should remain optional feature candidates until the user decides whether Micometeo should support the outing after choosing an area. [AllTrails introduction](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200401098516-Getting-started-on-AllTrails)

### Cited findings

- AllTrails supports nearby trail discovery, search, filters, map browsing, favorites and custom lists. It sends driving directions to another navigation app. On the trail it shows the planned route and current GPS position and records the activity. Its maps include topographic information. Trail reviews and photos are public. Its editors review submitted trails and edits before publication. [Getting started guide](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200401098516-Getting-started-on-AllTrails)
- Plus adds offline trails, parks and selected areas; 3D maps; printed maps; trail weather and conditions; popular-trail heatmaps; Garmin and COROS transfer and activity synchronization; wrong-turn alerts; live activity sharing; and member offers. Conditions include temperature, precipitation, snow depth, air quality and mosquito activity. [Membership guide updated 2026-09-28](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200882853140-The-benefits-of-AllTrails-premium-membership)
- Peak adds extra map layers, multi-day trip planning, route creation and editing, and camera identification for trees, flowers and insects with a logbook. The source does not establish mushroom identification. [Membership guide](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200882853140-The-benefits-of-AllTrails-premium-membership)
- The product has a free tier and annual paid tiers. Its US plans page shows USD 35.99 for Plus and USD 79.99 for Peak. The current support guide places community heatmaps in Plus, although older marketing and search results place them in Peak. Use the September 2026 guide for the current feature split. [Plans](https://www.alltrails.com/plans), [Updated support guide](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200882853140-The-benefits-of-AllTrails-premium-membership)
- Account controls offer public, followers-only and owner-only visibility for community lists, completed trails, activities, maps and saved lists. Changing defaults affects new content. Existing items have separate controls. Basic profile details remain visible. [Privacy options](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410205748244-Your-account-privacy-options)
- Users can request an export of all account data through the privacy centre. The published process can take up to 30 days. [Account export guide](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/18177547076116-How-to-request-a-copy-of-your-AllTrails-account-data)
- The service is available through a website, iPhone and Android apps. COROS documents route-file export and selection of GPX Track on desktop. [Privacy options across platforms](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410205748244-Your-account-privacy-options), [COROS integration guide](https://support.coros.com/hc/en-us/articles/37955063572500-Connect-AllTrails-with-COROS)

### Inferences

- The feature menu can distinguish choosing a mushroom area from downloading a map, reaching a trailhead, following a route, recording a walk and sharing live location. AllTrails documents these as separate capabilities. [Getting started guide](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200401098516-Getting-started-on-AllTrails), [Membership guide](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37200882853140-The-benefits-of-AllTrails-premium-membership)

### Gaps

- This is an outdoor-navigation reference, not evidence that Micometeo needs navigation or that AllTrails predicts porcini growth.
- Exact coverage of advanced layers, Italian subscription prices and moderation procedures beyond editorial trail approval were not established. The official route-export help URL failed to load, so GPX export relies on the device partner's instructions. No navigation or offline download was tested.
