# International mushroom prediction products

Research date: 2026-10-11. This inventory records public publisher claims. I read official websites and publisher app listings. I did not install apps, create accounts, buy access or test predictions in the field. Undocumented means the inspected sources do not settle the question. It does not mean a feature is absent.

## What do comparable products offer?

### Takeaway

The products combine maps with different sets of forecasts, explanations, saved places, trip records and field tools. Features below are candidates for the user's later selection. Their presence in another product does not make them requirements for Micometeo.

### Cited findings

#### Mycoverse

- Platform and coverage: iPhone and Android, developed in Norway for international use. The publisher says data quality varies by region. [Official site](https://mycoverse.app/en/)
- Prediction: choose species, area and day. Compare search areas and qualitative explanations. View rain over 3, 7 and 14 days, soil moisture, temperature, drying, forest, terrain, elevation, season, sunlight, slope and shade. Missing or coarse data is disclosed. [Official site](https://mycoverse.app/en/)
- Field features: Trip Mode, trail following, findings during trips, time and distance summaries, species watchlists, species library, offline profiles, field exercises and knowledge tests. Social features include group outings, companions, chat, approval, blocking and reporting. [Official site](https://mycoverse.app/en/)
- Access: the homepage advertises 10 free findings and three forecast days. Premium adds seven days, more species and areas, alerts, offline briefings and fuller explanations. Prices depend on the app store. [Official site](https://mycoverse.app/en/), [Product overview](https://mycoverse.app/en/mushroom-app/)
- Journal: photos, date, name, notes and location. Optional photo suggestions return possible matches. Findings are private by default. Public copies round positions to about 5 km. Private links allow approximate or exact positions with an expiry. Data export and account deletion are documented. [Privacy policy](https://mycoverse.app/en/privacy/)
- Safety trips: users can deliberately share their live route, last position and expected return through an expiring link. Ordinary Trip Mode remains on the device. [Privacy policy](https://mycoverse.app/en/privacy/)

#### ShroomCast

- Platform and coverage: iOS, Android and public web forecast pages. The website lists 15 European countries including Italy, with 14 species and a 14-day forecast. Four species are available free. [Official site](https://shroomcast.net/)
- Map and explanations: regional colors, daily playback, individual woodland cards, species estimates, 14-day rain, tree type and age, ground details, and explanations of the forecast and strength of its supporting records. [Method and features](https://shroomcast.net/about/)
- Weather and habitat: past rain, temperature, humidity, pressure, cloud, soil moisture, soil temperature, forest and season. The publisher describes models trained from reported finds and earlier weather. [Method and features](https://shroomcast.net/about/)
- Journal and privacy: record finds and unsuccessful searches. Compare records with forecasts. Saved woods stay on the phone. Submitted finds reach the server with their region rather than their woodland. Users can delete server records. [Method and features](https://shroomcast.net/about/)
- Other documented features: species trends, confirmed community finds, alerts, side-by-side species calendar, recent regions, favorites, advanced filters, offline mode, smooth forecast curves and comparisons with the previous year. Species pages name dangerous lookalikes. Premium unlocks all species, longer forecasts, alerts and filters. The store also claims to use moon phases. This is a publisher claim, not a research conclusion. [Publisher App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/shroomcast-mushroom-forecast/id6748039058)

#### Tattis

- Platform and coverage: iOS and Android, Finland. Free access includes 24 species, search, species cards, personal place markers and notes, cloud synchronization, submission to laji.fi and protected-area maps. [Official site](https://tattis.life/en/)
- Pro features: 39 species, separate species maps, forest-type layers, species weather, downloaded offline maps and alerts for season starts or favorable conditions. Prices displayed are EUR 3.99 monthly, EUR 12.99 yearly and EUR 69.99 lifetime. [Official site](https://tattis.life/en/)
- Method: forest inventory, soil, elevation, slope, water proximity, tree cover, vegetation and observations describe habitat. Recent rain, temperature and frost adjust it daily. Outside a species' fixed season, its forecast becomes zero. Early finds can still be logged. [Official site](https://tattis.life/en/)
- Score: the map multiplies habitat suitability, current weather and season. Yellow-to-red colors indicate increasing suitability. The publisher explicitly says a red area does not confirm mushrooms are present. [Official site](https://tattis.life/en/)

#### Fung.es

- Access: browser map and installable app, open without an account. The documentation discusses European and US environmental data, but does not give a complete coverage list. [Instructions](https://www.fung.es/instructions/)
- Features: species selection covering mushrooms and other edible plants, colored zones, 0-to-10 ratings, zone percentages and details, forecast slider, location button, five map styles, Google Maps destinations, nearby recipes and routes through ingredient locations. [Instructions](https://www.fung.es/instructions/)
- Offline: download a region containing maps and forecasts, valid for seven days. Species selection, forecast slider, details, styles and location remain available. Photo identification runs on the device after a model download. Routes and environmental detail retrieval need internet access. [Instructions](https://www.fung.es/instructions/)
- Inputs: weather, soil acidity, vegetation, host trees, elevation, water proximity, sightings and season. Scores concern availability of edible fruiting parts. They do not merely indicate that the organism lives there. [Instructions](https://www.fung.es/instructions/)

#### Chasseurs de Champignons

- Product: interactive website with colored search areas, account access and current location. France is the directly documented market in the inspected French pages. [Official site](https://www.chasseursdechampignons.com/fr/)
- Maps: species selection, tree and soil details, acidity, altitude, exposure and advanced habitat searches. Paid Cueillette Plus adds more than 15 other edible species, seven-day growth forecasts, alerts, detailed habitat at selected points, habitat mode and routes to areas. [Official site](https://www.chasseursdechampignons.com/fr/)
- Pricing: morel or porcini-and-chanterelle passes cost EUR 48 for one year. The current subscription offer says EUR 28 initially and EUR 5 monthly. A separate FAQ still mentions EUR 48 to restart, so the initial-fee wording conflicts. [Official site](https://www.chasseursdechampignons.com/fr/)
- Other features: gift passes, a public learning blog and an external Facebook community. The full one-year gift pass costs EUR 93. [Gift page](https://www.chasseursdechampignons.com/fr/offrez-une-carte)

#### Pilzwetter

- Product: Austrian browser map of recent rain and temperature. Users choose a period within the last 30 days, inspect colored rainfall areas and click locations for charts and lists. The interface includes seven-day and 15-day period shortcuts. [Official site](https://pilzwetter.at/)
- Data: 1 km weather grid from GeoSphere Austria radar and stations. Previous-day data is fetched around 07:00 daily. OpenStreetMap supplies the background map. The publisher says its analytics use no cookies or personal data. [Official site](https://pilzwetter.at/)
- Role: this is a documented weather exploration tool for mushroom hunters. The source does not document species probability calculations. [Official site](https://pilzwetter.at/)

#### Mushroom Forecast

- Evidence is limited to indexed official-page content. Direct fetching returned an empty page. The indexed page advertises a global interactive map across 108 regions, 81 species, location and place search, satellite and road maps, and Today, 3 Days and 7 Days choices. Each marker represents an approximate 5 km area. [Official site](https://mushroomforecast.com/)
- The displayed ranges run from below 15 percent to 85-100 percent. The page attributes the map to current weather, soil moisture and reported sightings. Premium claims include best-day forecasts, species timing, saved trip planning and monthly regional seasons. The inspected evidence does not establish exact prices or whether all advertised controls work. [Official site](https://mushroomforecast.com/)

#### Mappa dei Funghi, The Mycologist

- This Italian product was assigned as an exception to the international scope. It also links country sites for France, Belgium, Germany, Spain, Portugal, Switzerland, Austria and Czechia. Access works in phone, tablet and desktop browsers. [Italian official site](https://mappadeifunghi.it/)
- Features: species and area selection, habitat probability areas, daily growth index, seven-day trend, clicked-location rain, soil and elevation details, configurable alert thresholds, saved places, private find journal, photos and the weather on each find date. [Italian official site](https://mappadeifunghi.it/)
- Species offered are porcini, chanterelles and morels. The free demo currently covers Tuscany without an account. Inputs named include woodland, trees, geology, soil acidity, altitude, rain, temperature and soil moisture. [Italian official site](https://mappadeifunghi.it/)
- Pricing and access: one map means one species in one area for one year. Prices start at EUR 14.90 annually per map, with a three-for-two offer. Subscriptions renew yearly. Accounts provide cross-device access and subscription management. Changing species or area requires a new package. [Map plans](https://mappadeifunghi.it/mappe-complete/)

### Inferences

- Habitat maps, weather details, percentages, saved places, trip records and alerts solve separate needs. They should be selectable separately in the product discussion.
- Native apps demonstrate field features that a website could consider, but they do not establish that native apps are required.

### Gaps

- No paid or signed-in feature was tested.
- There is no basis for treating any marketing accuracy claim as independently measured performance.
- Exact spatial accuracy is undocumented for most products. Map zoom or a small colored area does not establish the detail of the weather evidence.

## What do the percentages actually mean?

### Takeaway

The products use different meanings for numbers. A percentage requires a specific event, area and date before users can interpret it consistently.

### Cited findings

- ShroomCast defines the regional percentage as the chance of active fruiting that day. Its woodland number is separately described as an estimate. [Official site](https://shroomcast.net/)
- Tattis describes a multiplied habitat, weather and season score. It does not document a percentage that was checked against repeated comparable searches. [Official site](https://tattis.life/en/)
- Fung.es describes favorable foraging conditions and availability of edible parts, with both a 0-to-10 map scale and zone percentages. [Instructions](https://www.fung.es/instructions/)
- The Mycologist separates habitat presence probability from the daily weather growth index and its seven-day trend. [Italian official site](https://mappadeifunghi.it/)
- Mycoverse describes qualitative signals and openly states when data is coarse or missing. [Product overview](https://mycoverse.app/en/mushroom-app/)

### Inferences

- The product decision is whether Micometeo predicts favorable growing conditions, actual fruiting somewhere in an area, or the chance a person finds porcini during a defined search. These are different promises.
- A visible percentage and a separate explanation of data quality are different features. A percentage alone cannot communicate both.
- A map for today and a map seven days ahead require separate decisions about their purpose and acceptable uncertainty.

### Gaps

- The inspected sources do not provide a common field-test method that makes competitor percentages comparable.
- No source here proves that 70 percent displayed by a product means success in seven out of ten comparable visits.

## Which evidence is incomplete or conflicting?

### Takeaway

The inventory is a sourced public-feature comparison, not a certification that every advertised feature works. Marketing pages, policy pages and app listings sometimes disagree.

### Cited findings

- Mycoverse's homepage describes 150 species, while its product overview says 100. Its store description says findings are never shared, while the privacy policy documents deliberate sharing. Treat species count and sharing wording as inconsistent documentation. [Homepage](https://mycoverse.app/en/), [Product overview](https://mycoverse.app/en/mushroom-app/), [Publisher Google Play description](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=no.mycoverse.app), [Privacy policy](https://mycoverse.app/en/privacy/)
- ShroomCast's website says 14 species and 15 countries. Older store copy says more than 10. Use the website for the current claimed totals, while retaining the store's documented feature descriptions. [Homepage](https://shroomcast.net/), [Publisher App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/shroomcast-mushroom-forecast/id6748039058)
- Mushroom Map's creator linked mushroommap.app and described US morel habitat and daily forecasts based on sightings, soil, trees, climate and recent weather. The linked service could not be fetched during this review, so its current feature inventory is unresolved. [Creator's public post](https://www.reddit.com/r/mushroom_hunting/comments/1js69fw/i_made_a_map_that_forecasts_where_and_when_morels/)
- ShroomSpy's inspected homepage is primarily a mushroom marketplace, with species and foraging material. A prediction product was not established by the inspected sources, so it is not counted as one here. [Homepage](https://shroomspy.com/)

### Inferences

- Privacy needs explicit choices about private places, approximate public positions, deliberate sharing and deletion. The existence of a community feature does not settle those choices.
- Terms such as offline mode need their own definition. Saved map tiles, the last forecast, a journal and navigation can have different connectivity requirements.

### Gaps

- Tattis does not clearly document a forward forecast duration on the inspected page. Daily updates concern current conditions.
- Fung.es does not clearly state its complete current species list or its forecast duration. Seven-day download validity is not proof of a seven-day forecast.
- Chasseurs de Champignons does not document offline use, private journaling or exports in the inspected sources.
- Pilzwetter does not document accounts, favorites, logs, alerts, offline use, paid plans or community functions in the inspected source.
- Mushroom Forecast has no verified app installation, offline support, privacy controls, journal, alerts or exact pricing in the inspected evidence.
- Mappa dei Funghi does not document offline use, exports, community posting or photo identification on the inspected pages.
