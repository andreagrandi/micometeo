# Italian mushroom websites and apps

Accessed on 2026-10-11. This inventory covers publicly documented features. I inspected publisher pages and app-store descriptions. I did not install apps, create accounts, buy access, or test predictions in the field. Availability below means that a public product page or store listing was reachable. A listed feature is a publisher claim unless stated otherwise. Unknown does not mean absent.

## Which services help decide where and when to look?

### Takeaway

Italian products already combine growth maps, weather, terrain, saved places, explanations and alerts. Their displayed numbers vary between condition scores, species estimates and claimed probabilities. These meanings should remain separate in Micometeo's feature choices. [Meteo Funghi method](https://meteofunghi.fungocenter.it/how-it-works), [ilCercaFunghi features](https://ilcercafunghi.it/funzionalita-mappa), [FungRadar FAQ](https://it.fungradar.com/domande)

### Cited Findings

#### FungRadar

- Web service for Italy, Ticino and San Marino. It publishes regional and town pages, seven-day indices, growth phases and explanations. Other sections cover mushroom species, berries, ticks, mosquitoes, sandflies and website badges. Its listed inputs include weather, satellite land cover, terrain, protected areas and GBIF observations. [Forecast directory](https://it.fungradar.com/previsioni)
- The homepage advertises a 2.5 km map, separate species models, weather from the preceding month, soil moisture, forest cover, elevation and slope. Users see reasons, reliability and labels where a region uses demonstration data. [Homepage](https://it.fungradar.com/)
- Saved places stay in the current browser without registration or device sync. Clearing browser data removes them. This page also describes voluntary public contributions rounded to roughly 250 square kilometres. [Saved places](https://it.fungradar.com/i-miei-posti)
- The FAQ says recalculation happens every six hours. Coverage quality varies, and detailed terrain currently covers one region. A gathering-permission layer is planned. Identification and edibility confirmation are explicitly excluded. The FAQ denies a collaborative sightings map, which conflicts with the saved-places page's description of shared contributions. [FAQ](https://it.fungradar.com/domande)
- The publisher exposes validation results and failures, including a weekly ranking result below its chosen target. These are publisher-reported results, not an independent audit or a calibrated percentage of mushroom presence. [Validation page](https://it.fungradar.com/come-verifichiamo)

#### Meteo Funghi by FungoCenter

- Italian web map, free national browsing, Google login, nightly updates, up to six monitored locations, selectable alert thresholds and morning email alerts. Pro adds today's local score, seven-day forecasts and a highlighted best day. The public alert example is explicitly simulated. No public price appeared on the landing page. [Product page](https://meteofunghi.fungocenter.it/)
- Its score runs from 0 to 10. It uses average temperature over seven days and weighted rain over 28 days, with OpenWeatherMap as the provider. It explicitly excludes forest type, altitude, exposure and other habitat factors. Marketing calls the colour a probability, but the documented calculation is a weather condition score. [Method and colour legend](https://meteofunghi.fungocenter.it/how-it-works)

#### MycoMap

- Nationwide web app with guest access, free accounts and Pro. Documented controls include location search, dated growth maps, a 0 to 5 score, point explanations, likely species, 40-day weather, 15-day score history, rain and hail, temperature, relief, satellite, trails, stations and downloadable areas. [App page](https://mycomap.it/)
- Its diary records photos, species, quantity, search duration, maturity, larvae, competing collectors, forest type, slope, soil moisture and notes. Zero-find trips record possible reasons. Photo metadata can fill date, elevation and location. Saved forests support trends and alerts. Groups offer invitations, posts, replies, shared records and forest notifications. The page also advertises GPS recording and GPX import/export, while another paragraph labels routes as coming soon. Field measurements are restricted to collaborators. [App page](https://mycomap.it/)
- Find coordinates are displayed as an approximate 250 m area, even to their owner. The publisher retains precise coordinates for improving predictions. GPS routes remain precise and private, with optional direct sharing. Accounts can be deleted in the app. [Privacy policy](https://mycomap.it/privacy)
- Pro prices are EUR 1.50 weekly, EUR 4.50 monthly, EUR 24 annually, or EUR 50 for a supporter year. Monthly and annual plans renew. The page still advertises free Pro until 15 September, so that promotion appears stale on the access date. [Plans](https://mycomap.it/piani)

#### Mappa dei Porcini Premium

- Android listing for Italy. Weather-station map updates five times daily and distinguishes four porcini types. Red means unsuitable, yellow preparatory conditions, green favourable production and blue declining production. A long press calculates local estimated temperature, accumulated rain and current growth conditions. Station details show the previous ten days. The description mentions personalising its separate prediction model for a user's forest. It explicitly warns that favourable weather does not establish that the matching porcini species lives there. The listing contains advertising and declares no data collection or third-party sharing. These declarations were not independently verified. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=it&id=it.matteoperseoinformatica.mappadeiporcinipremium)

#### ilCercaFunghi

- Italian web service advertising daily maps, more than 4,000 weather stations, 1 square kilometre cells, forest cover, slope exposure, rain totals, adjustable opacity and filters, local and station dashboards, and forecasts three days ahead. It combines rain remaining in the soil, temperature near roots, terrain, vegetation and species requirements. Public plans are Free, Esploratore at EUR 5 monthly and Ricercatore at EUR 10 monthly. The text extraction repeats restricted features under Free, so exact plan restrictions need visual confirmation. [Product and plans](https://ilcercafunghi.it/)
- Region selection and promising areas lead to point panels with separate Aereus and Edulis percentages, rain totals, coordinates, Google Maps navigation and detailed charts. Charts show changing growth potential and daily versus remaining useful rain. [Map features](https://ilcercafunghi.it/funzionalita-mappa)

#### MyBoletus

- Web service advertising all 20 Italian regions, daily updates and maps for porcini, chanterelles, Caesar's mushrooms and St George's mushrooms. Users select species and region. The map combines recent rain, night temperature variation, forest type and elevation. Other public features include a seasonal calendar, regional growth articles, a national bulletin, guides, a newsletter and several languages. Navigation links expose account, profile, community-board and ranking destinations, but their app pages returned only a shell, so functionality behind those links remains unverified. [Homepage](https://myboletus.com/it/)

#### Semaforo dei Funghi

- Android app from Funghi Magazine. Its national zoomable map updates weekly using weather and field reports. The app links the latest detailed forecast article, includes an illustrated species atlas, and provides live weather and accumulated rain from Italian stations. Historical station data can be downloaded as PDFs. Push notifications announce map and article updates. The publisher describes its map as indicative. The store declares no data collection or third-party sharing. No numeric probability definition or forward forecast horizon is supplied. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.funghimagazine.semaforodeifunghi)

### Inferences

- A feature comparison should distinguish a habitat map, today's conditions, future conditions, field sightings and private diary entries. Products expose these as different functions, even when marketing groups them under finding mushrooms. [FungoCenter method](https://meteofunghi.fungocenter.it/how-it-works), [ilCercaFunghi features](https://ilcercafunghi.it/funzionalita-mappa), [FungRadar saved places](https://it.fungradar.com/i-miei-posti)

### Gaps

- No independent accuracy audit was found for these products. FungRadar publishes its own evaluation, but that does not verify its live service or establish a calibrated probability.
- Diary, offline use, identification, community, export and alert support remain unknown for Mappa dei Porcini and ilCercaFunghi unless listed above.
- MyBoletus subscription rules, saved-place privacy, alerts, offline use, diary and detailed score meaning remain unknown.
- MycoMap's homepage conflicts with its privacy policy about storing exact find coordinates.

## Which apps add field recording, navigation and personal features?

### Takeaway

These products add substantial features beyond growth maps. Private diaries, offline navigation, notifications and identification aids need their own product decisions. [Mycel listing](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/mycel-mappa-dei-funghi/id6771742569), [MushScout plans](https://mushscout.eu/it/premium/)

### Cited Findings

#### Catacapea

- iPhone app for Italy with scores for 26 species, a map, ten suggested places near a chosen base, explanations, recent iNaturalist reports and personal finds. Inputs include forest, elevation, exposure, recent rain and temperature. It offers trails, relief, contours, an offline return compass, regional gathering rules, closed days, an illustrated guide, dangerous lookalikes and photo suggestions. Pro adds trip recording and downloaded maps. Base offers a 10 km search radius, today and tomorrow, three saved places and two monthly identifications. Pro offers a 60 km radius, all Italy, seven days, unlimited places and growth alerts. Base costs EUR 9.99 monthly or EUR 59.99 annually. Pro costs EUR 19.99 monthly or EUR 119.99 annually. A seven-day Base trial is advertised. Store text was retrieved through indexed publisher content after direct content retrieval failed. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/catacapea/id6817034469)

#### Mycel

- iPhone/iPad diary with Italian localisation and European coverage. Features include weather-based outing suggestions, offline maps, find markers, routes, notes, named areas, custom categories/species and worldwide GBIF sightings. Lock-screen and Dynamic Island controls record finds for three pinned species. Apple Watch shows trips. It includes over 60 reference entries and dangerous lookalikes. Photos stay in Apple Photos. Records use private encrypted iCloud or local-only storage. Mycel excludes its own photo identification but supports Apple Visual Look Up. Free includes two trips. Pro adds forecasts and bulk photo import. The description quotes EUR 39.99 lifetime and EUR 14.99 annually, but the purchase table shows EUR 44.99 and EUR 17.99. Monthly costs EUR 1.99. Regional packs and a web heatmap appear as future promises. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/mycel-mappa-dei-funghi/id6771742569)

#### FunghiMapp

- iPhone and iPad app with an Italian store description, although language metadata says English. It records private places for mushrooms, truffles and berries, with photos and notes. Harvest records include automatic weather. Seasonal reminders and offline maps are advertised. The pricing model is a free download with a EUR 24.99 lifetime unlock and no subscription. Store privacy declarations include precise location and user identifiers linked to the user. Geographic coverage is unspecified. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/funghimapp/id6749822174)

#### MushScout

- Android app with species-specific scores, twelve-day forecasts, explanations of missing rain or temperature, and an annual subscription. Users analyse a selected forest or draw its boundary. It highlights promising parts using weather and terrain. Diaries include GPS routes, finds and zero-find outings. It compares seasons, plans routes with walking estimates and sends a prepared trip message through the user. Return-time checks, emergency messages and photo suggestions are advertised. Free access has usage limits; watching adverts or using referrals expands some limits. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=it&id=it.fungofind.app)
- Other documented features include satellite and topographic maps, trails, GPS recording with the screen off, return-to-car navigation, nearby-find reminders, reconstructed past outings, photo and voice notes, seasonal recaps, anniversaries, private achievements, rubbish-collection records, friend challenges and shared live outings. Users can lend one forest to a friend, hide exact finds, opt into coarse community aggregates, correct local rain, export JSON/CSV/GPX, and compare their diary against recent weather. Forecast and map colouring have distinct meanings. [Product page](https://mushscout.eu/it/)
- Premium adds full forecasts for all saved forests, all 25 supported species, unlimited forest analysis, up to 20 monitored forests, detailed missing-condition explanations, forecast alerts, rain alerts, year comparisons, full diary statistics, voice notes, terrain shading, drainage features, 3D routes, forest type and offline use without the free limits. Free offers three followed species, three forest analyses, one monitored forest, twenty finds, ten trips, three photo suggestions daily and one map download. The publisher lists annual billing but leaves the price to Google Play. [Plan comparison](https://mushscout.eu/it/premium/)

#### FcP Map, Funghi che Passione

- Android and web product for Italy. Features include GPS, compass, altimeter, saved parking and return guidance, private pins with photos and habitat, cloud sync, a 0 to 100 local score, growth map, personal growth table, push alerts above 70, guided identification, live group tracking, separation alerts, diary and seasonal statistics, shared pins, SOS, detailed IGM maps, GPX/CSV export and a verified expert badge. Core maps and saved pins work offline; forecast updates and group tracking need a connection. Free includes five pins. Plus costs EUR 2.99 monthly or EUR 24.99 annually. Pro costs EUR 6.99 monthly or EUR 54.99 annually. Membership includes shop benefits. Online courses remain labelled as coming later. The score combines rain over 15 to 30 days, soil temperature, forest humidity, forest type and species. These are publisher claims; its Android listing exists, but I did not test the product. [Publisher feature description](https://funghichepassione.shop/blog/fcp-map-la-migliore-app-per-andare-a-funghi-in-italia/), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=it&id=shop.funghichepassione.fcpmap)

### Inferences

- Saving a place privately and using its coordinates to improve a shared model are separate choices. Mycel offers local-only storage, while MycoMap retains exact coordinates for predictions. [Mycel](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/mycel-mappa-dei-funghi/id6771742569), [MycoMap privacy](https://mycomap.it/privacy)
- Recording failed outings is a distinct feedback feature, not just a photo diary. MushScout and MycoMap both ask for it. [MushScout](https://mushscout.eu/it/), [MycoMap](https://mycomap.it/)

### Gaps

- No paid functions were tested. Pricing is the displayed Italian offer, with taxes, promotions and store differences unverified.
- Catacapea's score definition, exact location sharing and data retention need separate verification. Its listed language says English despite an Italian description.
- FunghiMapp does not document a predictive growth map, AI identification, community or export in the inspected description. Those features remain unknown.
- Mycel's catalogue translations and future versus released heatmap functions need clarification. Its description and purchase table disagree on annual and lifetime prices.
- MushScout has many more documented plan-level details than can fit in a compact competitor table. Its linked comparison is the primary checklist if the user selects diary, social, navigation or advanced forecast features.

## What else surfaced, and what remains uncertain?

### Takeaway

The names in this inventory identify distinct products. Similar names and translated brands should not be combined without checking the publisher. The research is broad, but it does not claim to cover every product on the market.

### Cited Findings

- Associazione Meteofunghi at meteofunghi.it is separate from FungoCenter's Meteo Funghi. Its public page presents a dated Italian mushroom-growth chart and Google Earth overlays. A municipality list is explicitly described as coming soon. [Association website](https://www.meteofunghi.it/)
- Mappa dei Funghi advertises The Mycologist, terrain and weather maps, seven-day trends, threshold alerts and a private diary. Coordinate with the international-product inventory before counting it twice. [Italian publisher page](https://mappadeifunghi.it/)
- Additional adjacent products surfaced in search: FungoApp for saved areas, offline maps, compass, rain history, three-day weather, calendars and visit statistics; Funghi italiani for AMINT's species reference and GPS records. They were not investigated deeply here. [FungoApp](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.fungoapp.app), [Funghi italiani](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/funghi-italiani/id1169188181)

### Inferences

- The useful choice is between capabilities, such as future estimates, private spots or group tracking, rather than copying a competitor's package wholesale.

### Gaps

- Focused searches did not establish an Italian mushroom product called FungoGo or an app specifically called Fungaiolo. Do not list these as verified competitors.
- Micodata was not established as a relevant consumer prediction product during this pass.
- The absence of a documented feature is not evidence that an app lacks it. Account-only functionality, reliability, accessibility, data portability and actual offline behaviour remain untested.
